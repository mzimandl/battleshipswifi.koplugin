# Changelog

Versions are the `version` in `_meta.lua`; since 1.1.6 each has a
[release](https://github.com/mzimandl/battleshipswifi.koplugin/releases) with
`battleshipswifi.koplugin.zip`.

## 1.1.7 – 2026-11-23

- KOReader no longer crashes when it quits or restarts (an update through
  ZenPM, for example) during a game or a search.
- On a Kindle, firewall rules left by a crash are not added twice.

## 1.1.6 – 2026-11-23

- Against the e-reader, its shot waits until the animation of yours has ended,
  so the ripples of a miss on the opponent's board are seen on Kindle too.
- No more black or white flash of the screen after every few animations.
- First version with a GitHub release to download.

## 1.1.5 – 2026-11-22

- The board size is picked in a *Board* submenu (10 × 10 or 12 × 12); shorter
  salvo label.

## 1.1.4 – 2026-11-22

- The fleet is picked in a *Ships* submenu, the game starts with *Start game*.

## 1.1.3 – 2026-11-22

- Menu: *Play against the e-reader* first, name and animations under
  *Settings*, *Join by address…* only in *Find a game*.
- The last address typed is no longer replaced by an old one on restart.

## 1.1.2 – 2026-11-22

- Sunk ships are crossed out in the overview of the opponent's fleet.

## 1.1.1 – 2026-11-22

- No leftovers of interrupted animations; a bigger opponent's board, the
  opponent and the score in the title.

## 1.1.0 – 2026-11-22

- Rules the host picks: a 12 × 12 board, salvo, ships that may touch
  (protocol version 4).
- Playing with keys: a cursor on the board, the screen's buttons in the Menu
  dialog.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-22

- Drag ships into place; mirror shaped ones (protocol version 3).

## 0.9.0 – 2026-11-22

- A game survives closing or restarting KOReader: *Continue the game against …*

## 0.8.0 – 2026-11-22

- Sunk ships are named; the score is kept across rematches, numbers after
  each game.

## 0.7.0 – 2026-11-22

- A network game goes on after sleep or a lost connection.

## 0.6.0 – 2026-11-22

- The opponent's fleet in small below their board.

## 0.5.0 – 2026-11-22

- Play against the e-reader, easy, normal or hard.

## 0.4.3 – 2026-11-22

- Smoke and other circles are drawn right on Kindle.

## 0.4.2 – 2026-11-22

- Faster refreshes after a shot; the board's outer edge stays.

## 0.4.1 – 2026-11-22

- No torn screen or stray marks during animations.

## 0.4.0 – 2026-11-22

- Richer shot animations; plain menu items for the fleet.

## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-21

- Shaped ships, new board graphics and shot animations (protocol version 2).

## 0.2.0 – 2026-11-05

- Renamed to `battleshipswifi` (before: `battleships`).

## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-04

- Battleships for two e-readers over Wi-Fi: host a game, find it on the
  network or join by address.
